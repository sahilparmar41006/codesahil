1. Gujarat – The Story of Dandi March

In 1930, Mahatma Gandhi led the famous Dandi March in Gujarat. He and his followers walked from Sabarmati Ashram to the coastal village of Dandi to protest the British salt tax.

After walking for about 24 days, Gandhi reached Dandi and broke the salt law by making salt from seawater. 2. Rajasthan – The Story of Maharana Pratap

Maharana Pratap was a brave ruler of Mewar in Rajasthan. He refused to accept the authority of the Mughal emperor Akbar and fought to protect his kingdom.

One of the most famous battles was the Battle of Haldighati in 1576. Although Maharana Pratap's forces were outnumbered, he continued fighting and later worked to recover many parts of his kingdom.
