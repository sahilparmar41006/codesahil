Gujarat

picnic places

  • 🌳 Indroda Nature Park – Gandhinagar Great for families, greenery, and nature.
  • 🌊 Nalsarovar Bird Sanctuary – Near Ahmedabad Perfect for boating, bird watching, and a peaceful picnic.
  • 💦 Hathni Mata Waterfall – Near Vadodara A beautiful monsoon destination surrounded by nature.
  • 🦁 Gir National Park – Junagadh Best for wildlife lovers and seeing Asiatic lions.
  • 🌿 Polo Forest – Sabarkantha Great for trekking, nature walks, and group picnics.
  • 🏞️ Saputara – Dang A hill station with pleasant weather, lakes, and scenic views.
  • 🌴 Dumas Beach – Surat Good for a relaxed outing with family or friends.
  • 🏰 Statue of Unity – Ekta Nagar Great for sightseeing, gardens, and a full-day trip.
  • 🌲 Purna Wildlife Sanctuary – Dang Ideal for a quiet picnic surrounded by forests.
  • 🎢 Tirupati Rushivan Adventure Park – Sabarkantha A fun option for families and children.
